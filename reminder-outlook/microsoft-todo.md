### **How to Use Microsoft To Do with Outlook for Reminders**
Microsoft To Do is a powerful task management app that integrates with Outlook. Here's how you can use it to set reminders.

---

### **1️⃣ Access Microsoft To Do**
- **Web:** [to-do.microsoft.com](https://to-do.microsoft.com/)
- **Windows App:** Pre-installed on Windows 10/11 or available in the Microsoft Store.
- **Mac/iOS/Android:** Download from the App Store or Google Play.
- **Outlook:** Integrated directly in Outlook under the **Tasks** or **To Do** tab.

---

### **2️⃣ Sync with Outlook**
Microsoft To Do automatically syncs with **Outlook Tasks** and **Flagged Emails** if you're using a Microsoft account (Outlook.com, Office 365, or Exchange).

1. **Sign in** to Microsoft To Do with the same account as your Outlook.
2. Ensure that **Tasks and Reminders** sync between **Outlook** and **Microsoft To Do**:
   - **Windows Outlook:** Go to `File` → `Options` → `Advanced` → Enable `Sync tasks with To-Do List`.
   - **Web Outlook:** Go to `Settings` (⚙️) → `View all Outlook settings` → `Tasks` → Enable Microsoft To Do integration.

---

### **3️⃣ Create a Task with a Reminder**
1. Open **Microsoft To Do**.
2. Click **+ Add a task** and type your task name.
3. Click on the task to open details.
4. **Set a Due Date:** Choose when the task is due.
5. **Set a Reminder:** Click `Remind me` and select a time for the notification.
6. (Optional) **Repeat the Task:** Click `Repeat` for recurring reminders.
7. **Syncs Automatically to Outlook!**

---

### **4️⃣ Flag Emails to Create Reminders**
- In **Outlook Desktop/Web**, **flag an email** 📌.
- It **automatically appears** in the **Flagged Emails** list in Microsoft To Do.
- You can **set a due date and reminder** in To Do for the flagged email.

---

### **5️⃣ Enable Notifications**
Ensure notifications are enabled so you don’t miss reminders:
- **Windows:** `Settings` → `System` → `Notifications` → Enable Microsoft To Do.
- **Mobile (iOS/Android):** Go to **App Settings** → Enable notifications.

---

### **6️⃣ Use Lists & Shared Tasks**
- Create **lists** to organize tasks (e.g., Work, Personal, Meetings).
- **Share lists** with teammates or family members for collaboration.
- Use **Smart Lists** (`Planned`, `Important`, `My Day`) to track reminders efficiently.

---

### **7️⃣ Advanced: Automate Reminders with Power Automate**
For advanced reminders:
- Use **Power Automate** to send automated emails, Teams messages, or push notifications for To Do tasks.
