### **How to Use OneNote with Outlook Integration**
OneNote integrates well with Outlook, allowing you to create tasks, set reminders, and sync notes across both apps. Here’s how to make the most of the integration.

---

## **1️⃣ Create Outlook Tasks from OneNote**
You can turn notes into actionable tasks in Outlook.

### **📌 Method: Add an Outlook Task from OneNote**
1. **Open OneNote (Windows Desktop or OneNote for Microsoft 365).**
2. Type a **task** in your notes.
3. Highlight the task and click on **"Outlook Tasks"** in the toolbar (under the **Home** tab).
4. Choose a due date (**Today, Tomorrow, Next Week, Custom**).
5. The task will now appear in:
   - **Microsoft To Do (Tasks section)**
   - **Outlook Tasks (Desktop/Web)**
   - **OneNote (with a task flag 🔴)**

✔ **Mark it Complete ✅** in OneNote or Outlook, and it updates across both.

---

## **2️⃣ Link OneNote Notes to Outlook Calendar Events**
You can link meeting notes to calendar events in Outlook.

### **📌 Steps:**
1. **Open Outlook Calendar** and select a meeting.
2. Click **"Meeting Notes"** in the ribbon.
3. Choose **"Take Notes in OneNote"** (if you’re the meeting organizer) or **"Share Notes with Meeting"** (for collaborative notes).
4. **OneNote opens a new page** with the meeting details.
5. **Save the page**—it’s now linked to your Outlook meeting.

✔ **Clicking on the OneNote link in Outlook will open the notes instantly.**

---

## **3️⃣ Email Notes from OneNote to Outlook**
Send notes directly to Outlook as an email.

### **📌 Steps:**
1. Open a **OneNote page**.
2. Click **"Email Page"** (Under the **Home** tab).
3. Outlook opens with the **OneNote content in the email body**.
4. Enter recipients and send.

✔ Useful for sharing **meeting notes, project updates, or reminders**.

---

## **4️⃣ Save Outlook Emails to OneNote**
You can save important emails to OneNote for easy reference.

### **📌 Method 1: Use the OneNote Button in Outlook (Desktop)**
1. Open an **email in Outlook**.
2. Click **"Send to OneNote"** in the ribbon.
3. Choose the **OneNote section** where you want to save it.
4. The email is saved as a **new page in OneNote**.

✔ Great for archiving **important discussions, receipts, or project emails**.

### **📌 Method 2: Forward Emails to OneNote via Email**
1. **Enable email integration**:
   - Go to [OneNote Email Settings](https://www.onenote.com/EmailSettings).
   - Set up your **Outlook email** to send to OneNote.
2. **Forward an email** to **me@onenote.com**.
3. The email appears as a **note in OneNote**.

✔ Works on **mobile and desktop**.

---

## **5️⃣ Automate OneNote and Outlook with Power Automate**
For advanced automation, use **Power Automate** to:
- **Automatically create an Outlook Task from a new OneNote page**.
- **Send email reminders when a task in OneNote is due**.
- **Sync meeting notes between Outlook Calendar and OneNote**.

**Steps:**
1. Go to [Power Automate](https://flow.microsoft.com/).
2. Search for **OneNote + Outlook** templates.
3. Choose and customize automation flows.
