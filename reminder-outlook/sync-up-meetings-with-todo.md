### **How to Sync Outlook Meetings with Your Microsoft To Do List**  
You can sync Outlook meetings with Microsoft To Do to manage your tasks and reminders efficiently. Here’s how:

---

## **1️⃣ Automatically Add Outlook Meetings to Microsoft To Do**
Outlook calendar meetings don’t automatically appear in Microsoft To Do, but you can **sync them manually or automate the process**.

### **📌 Option 1: Manually Create a Task from a Meeting**
1. Open **Outlook Calendar** (Desktop or Web).
2. **Right-click a meeting** → Select **"Follow Up" → "Add to Tasks"**.
3. The meeting details will appear in **Microsoft To Do** under "Tasks".
4. Set a **due date & reminder** if needed.

✔ **Great for important meetings that require follow-ups.**

---

### **📌 Option 2: Automate Sync Using Power Automate**
If you want **Outlook meetings to automatically sync to Microsoft To Do**, you can use **Power Automate**.

#### **Steps to Automate Meeting Sync**
1. **Go to** [Power Automate](https://flow.microsoft.com/).
2. Click **"Create a flow"**.
3. Select **"Automated cloud flow"**.
4. Choose **Outlook** as the trigger → Select **"When a new event is created in Outlook Calendar"**.
5. Add an **action** → Choose **Microsoft To Do** → **"Create a task"**.
6. Set:
   - **Task name:** Use Outlook event title.
   - **Due Date:** Use event date.
   - **Details:** Include event location, link, and notes.
7. Click **"Save"** → **Test your flow**.

✔ **Now, new Outlook meetings will appear as tasks in To Do automatically.**

---

## **2️⃣ Link a OneNote Page to a Meeting in Microsoft To Do**
1. **Create meeting notes** in OneNote (`Meeting Notes` in Outlook).
2. **Copy the OneNote page link**.
3. **Paste it into Microsoft To Do** under the corresponding task.

✔ **This helps you track meeting notes directly from your To Do list.**

---

## **3️⃣ View Flagged Emails (Meeting Invitations) in Microsoft To Do**
- **Flag a meeting invite email in Outlook** 📌.
- It will **appear in Microsoft To Do under "Flagged Emails"**.
- Add a **reminder** or **due date** if needed.

✔ **Useful when meeting invitations require actions.**

---

### **🔹 Best Use Cases**
✔ **Auto-sync Outlook meetings to To Do for reminders**  
✔ **Track meeting notes using OneNote & To Do**  
✔ **Flag meeting-related emails to create To Do tasks**
